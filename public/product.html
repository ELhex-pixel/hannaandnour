The fabric glides effortlessly across the skin, preventing friction and maintaining your hairstyle throughout the day. The generous dimensions allow for versatile styling options - from classic draping to elegant turbans.

Why You'll Love It

  • Buttery-soft feel with a subtle, natural sheen
  • Stays securely in place without pins (slip-resistant edges)
  • Breathable and lightweight - perfect for all seasons
  • UV protection of UPF 50+
  • Hypoallergenic and gentle on sensitive skin
  • Machine washable for easy care

Fabric Composition

70% Mulberry Silk, 30% Egyptian Cotton

  • Mulberry silk - known for its luxurious feel and natural temperature regulation
  • Egyptian cotton - long-staple fibers for exceptional softness and durability
  • Interlock weave for added strength without added bulk

Care Instructions

  • Hand wash cold or use the gentle cycle with garment bag
  • Use mild detergent - avoid bleach and fabric softeners
  • Lay flat to dry, away from direct sunlight
  • Iron on low setting while slightly damp
  • Store folded in a cool, dry place
  • Do not wring or twist the fabric
