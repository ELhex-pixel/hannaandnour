Privacy Policy

Home/Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

1. About this site

Hanna & Nour is a demonstration store. The checkout, account, cart and review features are illustrative: they do not process orders, collect money, or create real accounts. Payment fields accept test data only and are never transmitted anywhere.

2. Data we collect

This site is served as static pages and does not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. We collect no personal data server-side.

  • Language preference — stored only in your browser's local storage (hn-lang) to remember your choice of language on this device. It never leaves your browser.
  • Form entries (newsletter, reviews, checkout, profile) — processed in memory for demonstration only; nothing is stored or transmitted.

3. Cookies

We do not use cookies. No third-party tracking cookies are set.

4. Hosting

This website is hosted by Netlify. Netlify may process standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested pages) for security and performance. Their privacy policy applies to that processing.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR (EU) and UK GDPR, you may ask for access, correction, erasure or portability of your personal data. Since we hold no personal data, there is generally nothing to erase. For any question, contact us at care@hannaandnour.com.

6. Contact

Email: care@hannaandnour.com
