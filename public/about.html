Our Story

Where Faith Meets Fashion

Hanna & Nour — combining Hanna (grace) and Nour (light) — was born from a simple belief: that modesty is not a limitation, but a liberation. We exist to empower women who choose to cover - giving them fashion that honors their values without compromising their style.

Founded in 2021 by two sisters, Amina and Zahra, Hanna & Nour began as a small collection of handmade hijabs sold at local bazaars. Today, we serve thousands of women across the globe with pieces designed, crafted, and curated with love.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Modesty

We celebrate the beauty of hijab and modest dressing as a form of self-expression and devotion.

Quality

Every fabric is carefully selected. Every seam is intentionally placed. Every piece is crafted to last.

Empowerment

We believe every woman deserves to feel confident, beautiful, and unstoppable - in complete alignment with her beliefs.

Inclusivity

Hanna & Nour is for every woman, everywhere. We celebrate diversity in all its beautiful forms and sizes.

Craftsmanship

Made with Intention

Each Hanna & Nour piece begins as a sketch and a prayer. We work directly with artisan communities to source the finest fabrics - from ethically produced silks in Uzbekistan to hand-woven cottons in Egypt.

Our production partners are certified, fairly paid, and treated with dignity. We visit every workshop personally and know each artisan by name.
